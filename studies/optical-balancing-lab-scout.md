## 2. Helmut Leder — EVAlabs (Empirical Visual Aesthetics), University of Vienna

- **Why:** Leder et al. (2004) model of aesthetic appreciation (our bibliography); co-authored "Product aesthetics" with Hekkert (2008) — the rare design-bridging paper. Museum collaborations (Belvedere, Albertina, MAK/Sagmeister & Walsh *Beauty* exhibition) show applied comfort.
- **Lab page:** https://evalabs.univie.ac.at/
- **Fit:** the empirical-aesthetics establishment; large funded lab infrastructure; design-adjacent. Best bet for a prestige collaboration.

## 3. Johan Wagemans — Laboratory of Experimental Psychology, KU Leuven

- **Why:** leads the long-term Methusalem project on visual perception and art perception; the Gestalt-revision program (Wagemans et al. 2012) is the direct intellectual lineage of Arnheim's balance theory. Active, well-funded, art-perception native.
- **Lab page:** https://gestaltrevision.be/
- **Fit:** Gestalt lineage + art perception + active lab. Strong.

## 4. Ronald Hübner — Department of Psychology, University of Konstanz (emeritus)

- **Profile:** https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Ronald-Huebner
- **Fit:** ideal advisor/collaborator, but emeritus (professor 1998–2021) — approach as intellectual partner who may co-supervise rather than run the lab himself. Contact via ResearchGate or ronald.huebner@uni-konstanz.de (from his papers).
