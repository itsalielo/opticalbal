Optical balancing · adjustment task

What you will do

On each trial you will see two compositions side by side. Each composition contains two patches on a gray background. The left composition is fixed — it is the reference.

Worked example (static illustration — not interactive). The right-hand composition has one patch whose size you control.

  1. Look at both compositions.
  2. Change the size of the patch in the panel marked Adjustable — drag the slider, or use the ← → / + − keys.
  3. When the two compositions feel equally balanced, press Confirm (or Enter).

There is no right or wrong answer — go by feel. Take as long as you need on each trial.
