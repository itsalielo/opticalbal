# Best sources (mine) — curated for the future research project

The "better" tier: primary sources, full texts, and archives with the highest research value from the obscure-research rounds (2026-10-09). All free access. Full catalog with analysis at `~/workspace/design-sense/obscure-research-study.md`; deep-read notes at `~/workspace/design-sense/obscure-research-deep-study.md`.

## Full-text scans (archive.org)

- **Koch, The Book of Signs** — https://archive.org/details/bookofsigns0000rudo — 493 symbols drawn by Koch; the restraint law's pictogram ancestor.
- **Ruben, Die Reklame (1913/14)** — https://archive.org/details/ruben_diereklameihrekunstundwissenschaft_v_1_1913 — first German attempt to treat advertising as art *and* science, color plates.
- **Gebrauchsgraphik/novum 1950–63** — https://archive.org/details/novum_1950_21_6 — postwar German commercial art arguing itself toward corporate identity, month by month.
- **Ehmcke, Drei Jahrzehnte deutscher Buchkunst** — https://archive.org/details/dreijahrzehntede00ehmc — 1890–1920 German book art survey.
- **Koch, Schriftgiesserei im Schattenbild** — https://archive.org/details/McGillLibrary-rbsc_die-schriftgiesserei-im-schattenbilds_ColgateVIKlingspor-20547 — how a typeface is born at Klingspor, in silhouette.
- **Wendingen 1918** — https://archive.org/details/wendingen6mees — Wijdeveld's square-format first-year volume.
- **Typografia 1893** — https://archive.org/details/typografia1519unse — Czech printing-trade journal.
- **Campionario Nebiolo 1935** — https://archive.org/details/campionario-nebiolo-1935 — 346-page Italian type specimen.
- **Rodchenko, collected writings (1982)** — https://archive.org/details/1982_20211230 — primary theory of the movement that made restraint a moral position (Russian).
- **The Young Companion 良友** — https://archive.org/details/liangyou-1930.03 — ~180 issues of Shanghai's landmark pictorial (Chinese).
- **كتاب فن الطباعة (Bulaq, 1927)** — https://archive.org/details/AAlexandrina-144632 — earliest Arabic printing-craft manual found in full text.
- **Hannash, الخط العربي وحدود المصطلح الفني** — https://archive.org/download/alkhatt_al-arabi/alkhatt_al-arabi.pdf — terminology study of Arabic calligraphy; precision of naming precedes precision of form.
- **Afifi, Teaching Arabic Calligraphy** — https://archive.org/details/3_20210303_20210303 — full instructional scans.
- **Naji Zayn al-Din, Encyclopedia of Arabic Calligraphy** — https://archive.org/download/20260705_20260705_0518/كتاب%20موسوعة%20الخط%20العربي%20خط%20الثلث%20عربي%20بحث%20_text.pdf — multi-volume khatt encyclopedia.
- **form+zweck 1989-2** — https://dn721606.ca.archive.org/0/items/form-und-zweck/Form%2BZweck%20%E2%80%93%201989-2_text.pdf — DDR design journal; the "offenes Prinzip" ethic.

## Archives & institutions

- **HfG Ulm: 21 Rückblicke** — http://wordpress-cluboffulm.p501812.webspaceconfig.de/Curdes_HFG_Ulm-21_Rueckblicke_2006_%20e-book.pdf — 21 alumni essays; where "restraint as law" was first institutionalized (German).
- **Typografische Monatsblätter research archive** — http://www.tm-research-archive.ch — 80 years (1933–2014) of Swiss-German typographic debate, searchable (German).
- **Les Maîtres de l'affiche (Gallica/BnF)** — https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Ma%C3%AEtres_de_l%27affiche — 1895–1900 poster anthology, 256 plates; curatorial restraint, four posters a month (French).
- **Fujiwara lettering books, NDL Digital** — https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/12705016 — 1925/1926 prewar Japanese hand-lettering; indigenous kana logic before the Western grid (Japanese).
- **Archiv für Buchgewerbe index (Wikisource)** — https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Archiv_f%C3%BCr_Buchgewerbe_und_Gebrauchsgraphik — map to free scans of the Leipzig trade journal 1922–43 (German).
- **Magyar Grafika 1928 (EPA)** — https://www.epa.hu/02500/02506/00066/pdf/EPA02506_magyar_grafika_1928_01-02.pdf — printers' trade paper carrying Kassák's radical typography theory (Hungarian).

## Arabic & Maghrebi theory

- **الخط المغربي: تاريخ وواقع وآفاق** — https://bookslibrary.com/c-Islamic-calligraphy-download-3 — scholarly survey of the Maghrebi letterform tradition.
- **Melehi, فنان ومكان** — https://dafbeirut.org/sites/default/files/pubpress_docs/فنانون-ومكان-محمد-المليحي-وأصيلة.pdf — designer-as-publisher practice; constraint-driven masterclass.
- **One Dimension Manifesto — academic paper** — https://iasj.rdd.edu.iq/journals/uploads/2026/05/16/a8835de67e89c4917e00d5f46b4fd13b.pdf — analysis of Al Said's 1971 manifesto theorizing reduction itself.
- **Bulaq → Monotype Arabic (IJODH)** — https://www.ijodh.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/IJODH-0201-06.pdf — Arabic metal type from Bulaq to Monotype.
- **Ashour Arabic typography books** — https://ashour.orgfree.com/books.htm — free Arabic books on typography/calligraphy.
