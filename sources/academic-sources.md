# Academic research sources

Journals, thesis repositories, databases, and discovery infrastructures for the optical-balancing research project. Extracted from the design-skills repo (2026-10-09).

## Academic research sources (2026-10-09)

From the optical-balancing research project — the seed of the future research project's source base. Full landscape at `~/workspace/design-sense/optical-balancing-study.md` (§7) and `~/workspace/design-sense/optical-balancing-sources.md`.

**Design-research journals (open access):**
- **She Ji** — https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/she-ji-the-journal-of-design-economics-and-innovation — diamond OA, peer-reviewed design theory/methodology; the single most useful venue for the problematic.
- **Artifact** — https://intellectdiscover.com/content/journals/ajdp — diamond OA practice-based design research.
- **Visible Language** — https://journals.uc.edu/index.php/vl/about — OA visual-communication research since 1967.
- **InfoDesign** — https://infodesign.org.br/infodesign/about — OA Brazilian information design, trilingual.
- **International Journal of Design** — http://www.ijdesign.org — OA to read, indexed SCI-E/SSCI.
- **Sciences du Design** — https://sciences-du-design.org — French peer-reviewed design research, OA after 2-year wall.
- **w/k** — https://between-science-and-art.com — bilingual German/English OA, science–art interface.

**Vision & perception journals (open access):**
- **i-Perception** — https://doaj.org/toc/2041-6695 — SAGE OA perception science; natural home for balance-perception experiments.
- **Journal of Vision** — http://jov.arvojournals.org/ss/about.aspx — fully OA vision mechanics.

**Thesis repositories:**
- **NDLTD Global ETD Search** — https://ndltd.org/resources/find-etds/ — 1M+ theses worldwide; the DART-Europe successor (DART closed Feb 2025).
- **EThOS** — https://www.bl.uk/collection/ethos — UK doctoral theses; relaunched 2026 as metadata-plus-links after the 2023 cyberattack.
- **PQDT Open** — ProQuest open-access US dissertations, free to read.

**Databases & discovery:**
- **Google Scholar** — https://scholar.google.com — broadest net, citation chaining.
- **Semantic Scholar** — https://semanticscholar.org — AI literature mapping, 200M+ papers.
- **OpenAlex** — https://openalex.org — free open knowledge graph, systematic searches.
- **BASE** — https://www.base-search.net — 240M+ docs, OA ranked first.
- **CORE** — https://core.ac.uk — OA aggregator, finds legal OA versions of paywalled papers.
- **DOAJ** — https://doaj.org — authority for verifying genuine OA journals.

**Non-English discovery:**
- **HAL** — https://hal.science — French national open archive.
- **J-STAGE** — https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp — Japanese science journals, many free.
- **CiNii Research** — https://cir.nii.ac.jp — Japanese academic papers and books.
- **CyberLeninka** — https://cyberleninka.ru — large Russian OA article library.

## Academic research sources — Round 2 additions (2026-10-09)

From the optical-balancing Round 2 (missing experiment, non-English literature, 2024–2026 frontier). Zero URL duplicates with the above.

**More OA infrastructure:**
- **Unpaywall** — https://unpaywall.org — finds legal OA versions of paywalled papers; the fastest route past paywalls.
- **Internet Archive Scholar** — https://scholar.archive.org — full-text scholarly search over archived papers.
- **OpenEdition** — https://www.openedition.org — French humanities/social-science OA publishing.
- **OpenAIRE** — https://www.openaire.eu — European research outputs, OA-mandated.
- **EZB Regensburg** — https://ezb.uni-regensburg.de — electronic journals library, strong for German titles.
- **theses.fr** — https://theses.fr — French doctoral theses portal.
- **DiVA** — https://www.diva-portal.org — Scandinavian theses and publications.

**Journals that keep publishing balance/weight work:**
- **Symmetry (MDPI)** — https://www.mdpi.com/journal/symmetry — OA; published VME (2025), Lu et al. (2024), Teixeira et al. (2026) — the venue where balance-metric papers currently land.
- **Frontiers in Psychology** — https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology — OA; Hübner & Fillinger (2016) and the perception-science stream.
